Privacy Policy — GNIS

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

GNIS ("Reverse Karaoke") is a mobile game developed by Masami Kawamoto. GNIS does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require account creation and has no access to names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, contacts, photos, or recordings. GNIS does not use any third-party services to collect or process user data.

Microphone Access

GNIS requests access to your device's microphone solely to enable reverse karaoke gameplay. Audio is processed locally on your device and is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

Children's Privacy

GNIS does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
